# Stardew-Style HUD

An always-on HUD in the style of Stardew Valley, plus a toolbar for switching tools and items without opening the
pause menu.

**Use "Manual Download"** and drag `hm64_stardew_hud.nrm` onto the game window. r2modman / the Thunderstore mod
manager isn't supported by Harvest Moon 64: Recompiled.

## Features

- **Clock panel (top-right):** day, date, season, weather, the time in half-hour steps, and your gold.
- **E (energy):** your stamina. It turns yellow, then red, as it drains, and grows taller as your maximum stamina
  increases. Tools stop working when it runs out.
- **H (health):** HM64's hidden fatigue meter. It drains when you use tools at night or in rain or snow, turns yellow,
  then red, as you overwork, and if it empties you wake up sick the next morning. Sleep early and eat to recover.
- **Toolbar (bottom):** your tools on top and your items below, with your hand slot first. Seed bags show how many
  uses are left, and the watering can shows its water.
- Hidden on the title screen, in menus, and during cutscenes.

## Controls

| Button | Keyboard (default) | Action |
|---|---|---|
| D-pad Up / Down | I / K | Choose the tools row or the items row |
| D-pad Left / Right | J / L | Swap the next tool or item into your hands |

The items row also stops on empty hands, which puts the held item away. Switching only works while you're free to
act (not mid-action, in a menu, conversation, or cutscene, or on horseback).

## Options

- **Show HUD:** show or hide the whole HUD.
- **Show Toolbar:** show or hide the toolbar (also turns D-pad switching off).
- **Show Health Bar:** show or hide the H bar.
- **HUD Scale:** make the HUD bigger or smaller.
